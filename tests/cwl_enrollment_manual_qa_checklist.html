Section 1 · Root-Cause Fix

Family-wide player pool (Rule b)

The bug that started this whole redesign: a member with accounts in several family clans only got DMed for the ones in a clan someone remembered to check.

Setup: in Configure Participating Clans, leave at least one family clan unchecked, and confirm your test account has a linked player in that unchecked clan and in a checked one.
  1. Click Start Enrollment for the season.Summary's "Seeded" count includes the unchecked clan's current members, not just the checked clan's.
  2. Check the test account's DMs.One enrollment DM per linked player — including the one in the unchecked clan.
  3. Open Manage Enrollment.The unchecked clan's members appear under Unassigned (there's no column for a non-participating clan) — not missing entirely.
  4. Confirm a player from a genuinely unrelated clan (not in this guild's family at all) still does not appear anywhere.Pool is family-wide, not "everyone the bot has ever seen."

Section 2 · Reliability

DM retry and failure reporting

A Discord error for one recipient must never silently kill the rest of the batch, and the admin should always learn who wasn't reached.

  1. Run Start Enrollment against a normal-sized pool.Every DM-able recipient gets contacted; the summary's "Contacted" count matches.
  2. Pick one test account, disable "Allow DMs from server members" for this server (or block the bot), then run Start Enrollment again with them in the pool.Summary lists that account under a "Blocked" line by name — and everyone else in the pool still got contacted normally.
  3. Re-enable "Allow DMs from server members" (or unblock the bot) for that account.Nothing to see yet — just clears the way for the next step.
  4. Click Notify New Pool Members (Hub message or /clan management).That account now receives a fresh Confirm/Opt Out DM — a blocked recipient is never marked as already-DMed internally, specifically so re-enabling DMs and retrying via this button reaches them (project owner's spec, 2026-08-19). Nobody who was already successfully contacted gets a duplicate DM.

Section 8 · New feature

Guest-player provenance (right-click removal)

A guest player added because their whole clan was invited can only be removed by removing that clan — never individually, from either the board's context menu or the Remove Guest Players panel.

Setup: one participating family clan, one guest clan with a couple of real current members, and one player individually invited as a guest via search (not a member of the guest clan).
  1. On Manage Enrollment, right-click the card of the individually-invited guest player.A small context menu appears with a single "Remove guest player" option.
  2. Click it.The player disappears from the board shortly after (footer status briefly shows "Removed"). No page reload needed.
  3. Right-click the card of one of the guest CLAN's real members (guest-badged, same as any other guest).Same context menu appears — it looks identical, there's no visual difference between the two kinds of guest.
  4. Click "Remove guest player" on that clan member.An inline error in the footer explains they were added by the guest clan and only removing the whole clan removes them — the card stays put, nothing is deleted.
  5. Click elsewhere on the page (or press Escape) while the context menu is open, without choosing an option.The menu closes with no action taken.
  6. Open Configure Participating Clans → Remove Guest Players….Only the individually-invited guest player(s) are listed — the guest clan's real members do NOT appear in this list at all, even though they're guest-badged on the board.
  7. In the Guests search box, search for and try to Add a player who is already a current member of the guest clan you invited.A clear error explains they're already in the pool via that guest clan and can't be added individually — no duplicate entry appears on the board.
  8. Individually invite a guest player who is not yet in any guest clan on the roster. Confirm they show up in Remove Guest Players….Listed, same as any other individually-invited guest.
  9. Now invite that same player's real clan as a guest clan too, and Save.Reopen Remove Guest Players… — that player has disappeared from the list (their provenance flipped to clan-derived the moment their clan joined the roster, no action needed on the player itself). Right-clicking their board card and choosing Remove now gives the same "added by the guest clan" error as step 8-4.This is race condition 2 from the spec — "clan invitation beats individual invitation."
